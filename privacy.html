Vixi Privacy Support

Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

In short: Vixi does not collect, transmit or sell any of your data. Everything you add stays on your device, and nobody, including us, can see it.

Data we collect

None. Vixi has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your achievements, journal entries, connections, settings and any photos you attach are stored locally on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are removed when you delete the app.

Permissions

  • Photos: only when you choose to attach a photo. The selected photo is copied into the app's storage on your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / device biometrics: optional app lock. Authentication is handled by your device's operating system; Vixi never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications: optional reminders and anniversaries. They are scheduled locally on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your device's settings.

Backups

You can export a backup file and save or share it wherever you choose, for example to your files or cloud storage. Once exported, the file is handled by the service you chose and its privacy policy. Vixi only reads a backup when you explicitly import it.

Third parties

Vixi does not share data with third parties because it does not collect any.

Children

Vixi does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Vixi changes how data is handled, for example by adding sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: vixi.app@proton.me
