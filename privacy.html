Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

In short: Vixi has no servers and we never receive your data. Everything you add stays on your device and, only if you turn on Google Drive sync, in a private app folder in your own Google Drive. We don't sell, share or analyze your data.

Data we collect

We, the developers of Vixi, don't collect any data. Vixi has no Vixi account, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The only network connections Vixi makes are to Google, and only when you turn on Google Drive sync.

Data stored on your device

Your achievements, journal entries, connections, settings and any photos you attach are stored locally on your device, inside the app's private storage. They are removed when you delete the app.

Google Drive sync and Google user data

Sync is optional and off by default. If you sign in with Google and turn it on, Vixi requests access to the following Google user data:

  • Google Drive app data folder (drive.appdata scope): a hidden folder in your Google Drive that only Vixi can read and write. Vixi uses it solely to store and sync your Vixi data (achievements, journal entries, connections, settings and attached photos) between your devices. Vixi cannot see, read or change any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Your Google account email address: used only to show which Google account sync is connected to. It is stored only on your device.

How it's used: only to provide sync to you. Your data travels directly between your device and Google over an encrypted connection; it never passes through or is stored on servers operated by us.

Sharing: we don't transfer, sell or share Google user data with anyone. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models. No human reads it.

Limited Use: Vixi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion: synced data stays in your Google Drive until you delete it. You can turn off sync in Vixi's settings, delete Vixi's app data from Google Drive (Drive settings → Manage apps → Vixi → Delete hidden app data), and revoke Vixi's access at any time in your Google Account settings. Signing out removes the stored email address from your device.

Permissions

  • Photos: only when you choose to attach a photo. The selected photo is copied into the app's storage on your device.
  • Face ID / Touch ID / device biometrics: optional app lock. Authentication is handled by your device's operating system; Vixi never receives your biometric data.
  • Notifications: optional reminders and anniversaries. They are scheduled locally on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

You can revoke any permission at any time in your device's settings.

Backups

You can export a backup file and save or share it wherever you choose, for example to your files or cloud storage. Once exported, the file is handled by the service you chose and its privacy policy. Vixi only reads a backup when you explicitly import it.

Third parties

We don't share any data with third parties. If you turn on sync, your data is stored by Google in your Google account under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Vixi does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of Vixi changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
