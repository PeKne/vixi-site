Vixi Privacy Support

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to us.

vixi.app@proton.me

Please include your device model and Vixi version (shown in Settings).

Where is my data?

Only on your device. Vixi has no account and no servers, so we can't see or recover your entries.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

In Settings, export a backup and save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone (for example Files or cloud storage). On the new phone, install Vixi and import that backup in Settings.

I deleted the app. Can I get my entries back?

Only from a backup you exported earlier. Deleting the app removes all of its data from the device.

I can't unlock the app

The app lock uses your device's Face ID, Touch ID or biometrics. If authentication keeps failing, check that biometrics work in your device settings and that Vixi is allowed to use them.
