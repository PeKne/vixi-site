Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to us.

vixi.app@proton.me

Please include your device model and Vixi version (shown in Settings).

Where is my data?

On your device and, if you turn on sync, in a private app folder in your own Google Drive. Vixi has no servers, so we can't see or recover your entries.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Turn on Google Drive sync on both phones with the same Google account. Without sync, export a backup in Settings, save it somewhere you can reach from the new phone (for example Files or cloud storage), and import it in Settings on the new phone.

How do I stop syncing and remove my data from Google Drive?

Turn off sync in Vixi's settings. To delete the synced copy, open Google Drive settings → Manage apps → Vixi → Delete hidden app data. You can also revoke Vixi's access in your Google Account settings.

I deleted the app. Can I get my entries back?

Yes, if you had Google Drive sync on: reinstall Vixi and sign in with the same Google account. Otherwise only from a backup you exported earlier.

I can't unlock the app

The app lock uses your device's Face ID, Touch ID or biometrics. If authentication keeps failing, check that biometrics work in your device settings and that Vixi is allowed to use them.
