Vixi

A private app for remembering what you've done, what you've lived and who you've kept in touch with.

Achievements

Keep the things you've done, with dates, categories and photos, and see what happened on this day in past years.

Journal

Write down what you've lived and let Vixi bring old entries back when they matter.

Connections

Remember who you've kept in touch with, and who you haven't heard from in a while.

Private by design. Vixi has no account and no servers. Everything you write stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
