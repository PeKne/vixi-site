Vixi

A private app for remembering what you've done, what you've lived and who you've kept in touch with.

Achievements

Keep the things you've done, with dates, categories and photos, and see what happened on this day in past years.

Journal

Write down what you've lived and let Vixi bring old entries back when they matter.

Connections

Remember who you've kept in touch with, and who you haven't heard from in a while.

Sync with Google Drive

Optionally sign in with Google to keep your Vixi data in sync across your devices. Vixi stores it in a private app folder in your own Google Drive, which only Vixi can access. Vixi does not see or touch any of your other Drive files.

Private by design. Vixi has no Vixi account and no servers of its own. Your data stays on your device and, only if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. We never receive it. Read the privacy policy to see exactly which Google data Vixi uses and why.
